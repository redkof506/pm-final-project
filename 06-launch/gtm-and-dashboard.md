## Go-to-market summary

_A concise overview of your launch plan: channels, launch size, and overall strategy._

We're launching **Mood-Based Entry Point** (pick a mood on login to filter the rail) as an **Engagement**-focused feature for StreamLine's active users (logged in ≥1x/14 days) who drop off between homepage visit and content start. The launch is staged across three phases, starting at Tier M and scaling to **Tier L, Multi-channel**: Phase 1 (Beta, wk 1–8) rolls out via owned channels only — existing-user email and a new onboarding learning path — with no paid spend; Phase 2 (Launch) escalates to Tier L only if Phase 1 clears the engagement guardrail; Phase 3 (Full campaign) adds paid social (TikTok and other platforms) for a full multi-channel push. The PM owns release and tracking, Marketing owns advertisement strategy and communications, and Development owns ongoing tracking and issue resolution, with costs covered from the project baseline. Power users (top X% by weekly watch hours) are watched as a guardrail audience — the launch is not targeting them, and success requires not moving this group negatively.
